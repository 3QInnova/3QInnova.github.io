Support

The AI can blame you. Support won’t.

Contact a human

For help, privacy requests, accessibility feedback, or an inappropriate ad, email Support.exeptionHuman@3qinnova.com. This inbox is operated by 3QInnova LLC. We do not promise a particular response time.

Tell us the app version, device model, operating-system version, level, and what happened. Screenshots are optional: crop out notifications, names, and other private information. Never send passwords, payment details, or an Apple or Google account login.

Report an ad

Pause the game, open Settings & help, and choose Report an ad. Review the prepared report, choose your email app, and send it to Support.exeptionHuman@3qinnova.com. Nothing is sent automatically. If available, the report includes the ad response reference, placement, time, and build version. Add what was wrong; a screenshot is optional. You can also use an ad’s own information or AdChoices control.

Progress, sound, and offline play

Progress, earned Tokens, cosmetics, and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account or publisher-operated cloud save. Deleting the app can erase progress; we cannot restore a save we do not hold. An operating-system backup may behave differently from a fresh install.

Gameplay works without an internet connection. Ads and privacy-choice checks require a connection. If sound stops, open Audio and use its test/reconnect control, then check device volume. Returning from another app leaves the game paused until you resume.

Privacy choices

Open Pause → Settings & help → Ad privacy & info to review the choices available from Google. You can keep playing if ads cannot load or a privacy check fails. Choosing non-personalized ads does not mean that no data is processed.

Privacy policy

This policy covers the EXCEPTION: HUMAN iOS and Android game, its support messages, and this product information page. The operator is 3QInnova LLC. Contact: Support.exeptionHuman@3qinnova.com.

Game data on your device

Campaign progress, scores, earned Tokens, cosmetic choices, audio/control settings, and remark history are stored locally. The game does not upload these saves to us. Your device or backup provider may include app data in backups under your settings. There is no game account, chat, user upload service, or publisher-run gameplay analytics service in this version.

The fictional AI’s dialogue is authored game content. The game does not send your play to a generative AI service. We do not request access to your contacts, microphone, photos, or device location.

Advertising and Google

The iOS and Android apps include Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform for banner advertising and privacy choices. They may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app/device identifiers, ad views and interactions, consent choices, and diagnostic/performance information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google and advertising providers may process data outside your state or country.

We request non-personalized ads, disable Google’s publisher first-party ID and automatic collection through device location permissions, and, on iOS, do not request Apple’s tracking permission or access to IDFA. Android advertising and app-set identifiers may be processed by Google. Other identifiers and IP-derived information may still be processed. These settings are not a promise of zero data collection. Development builds may show Google test ads, which can still contact Google.

See Google’s Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and its partner-site/app explanation at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites for its processing, retention, and controls. Advertising partners’ practices can depend on your choices and the ad served.

Your ad choices

At launch, the app checks Google’s consent service and presents a privacy message when required. Open Pause → Settings & help → Ad privacy & info to revisit available choices, including applicable US-state opt-out choices. Your choice is not permission to contact you for marketing. If the consent check fails, the app leaves ads off and gameplay available.

We do not sell game saves or support correspondence. Advertising data sharing may qualify as sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under some state laws. Use the ad privacy controls to make the choices offered for your location, or contact us for help. Non-personalized advertising and a state-law opt-out are not necessarily the same setting.

Support and ad reports

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until removed by the app/device storage controls or deletion of the app; offloading an iOS app can retain its data. Deleting the app does not delete separate support emails or third-party ad records. Backup copies are subject to your backup provider’s controls.

We retain support correspondence while needed to resolve a request, address related follow-up or disputes, and satisfy legal obligations, and delete or de-identify it when no longer needed. Retention depends on the request and any legal hold. Google and the website/email providers apply their own retention rules; we cannot promise a deletion date for data they independently control.
