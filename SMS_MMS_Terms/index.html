1. About 3QInnova

3QInnova LLC is a Colorado technology consulting and engineering company. We provide technology consulting, software development, cloud and AI engineering, telecommunications and contact-center integrations, and interactive product development.

Our messaging program supports our own business communications and consent-based demonstrations or testing of communications technology.

2. SMS and MMS messages you may receive

When you choose to participate in an applicable 3QInnova SMS/MMS messaging program, messages may include:

  • Responses to your inquiries and requests for technical or consulting assistance.
  • Scheduling, project coordination, and service-related updates relevant to your relationship with 3QInnova.
  • Demonstration and test messages when you have specifically agreed to participate in a 3QInnova lab or technology demonstration.

Any future promotional messaging program would require its own appropriate consent and disclosures. Consent to service messages or lab testing is not blanket consent to marketing messages.

3. Consent and eligibility

The contact form prepares an email with your request and consent choices. You must send that email to complete the request. 3QInnova must review it and retain the applicable consent before enrolling your number. The form does not automatically subscribe you or send a text.

Participation is voluntary. Before enrolling you in a messaging program, 3QInnova will ask for your affirmative consent through the applicable sign-up or enrollment process. That process will identify 3QInnova, describe the messages, and provide the applicable terms and privacy information.

You must own the mobile number you provide or have authorization to use it. Let us know if your number changes or is reassigned. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing services. Reading this page, visiting our website, or providing a number for another purpose does not by itself enroll you in recurring text messages.

4. Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your inquiries, active services, and any demonstration or test session you join. Any program-specific message limit will be disclosed when you enroll.

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge for sent or received messages under your plan. Contact your carrier for information about your messaging or data charges.

5. How to stop messages

Reply STOP to a message from the program's sending number to cancel further messages from that program. A final confirmation message may acknowledge your request. After opting out, you will not receive further program messages unless you affirmatively opt in again.

You can also request assistance with an opt-out by emailing will@3qinnova.com. Include enough information to identify the applicable messaging program. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

6. Help and support

Reply HELP to the program's sending number for assistance, or contact will@3qinnova.com. You can also visit 3qinnova.com to learn about our services and contact us.

These terms describe the intended program policy. The enrollment disclosures and STOP/HELP handling for each sending number must match the program before it is activated.

Read our standalone Privacy Policy for more information about website, business communication, and SMS data.
