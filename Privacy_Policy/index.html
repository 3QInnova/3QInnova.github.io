1. Who we are and what this covers

3QInnova LLC is a Colorado technology consulting and engineering company. Our work includes consulting, software development, cloud and AI engineering, telecommunications and contact-center integrations, and interactive products.

This draft policy covers information shared through this website, inquiries to 3QInnova, and our own messaging programs and consent-based technology demonstrations. A client engagement or separate application may have its own agreement or privacy notice.

2. Information we may collect

  • Information you provide: your name, business contact details, email address, phone number, and the content of inquiries or support requests.
  • Project information: information you choose to provide about your organization, requirements, scheduling, or services you request.
  • Messaging information: the mobile number used to communicate with us, message content, consent records, opt-out preferences, timestamps, and delivery or troubleshooting records.
  • Technical information: hosting and communications providers may process IP addresses, browser or device information, request logs, and similar information needed to deliver and protect their services.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive information through ordinary email or SMS.

3. How we use information

We intend to use information to respond to inquiries, discuss or deliver requested services, coordinate projects, provide support, operate authorized demonstrations, and maintain reliable communications.

We also use relevant records to manage messaging consent and opt-outs, investigate delivery issues or misuse, protect our systems, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Information provided for one purpose does not automatically authorize unrelated marketing.

4. SMS, mobile numbers, and consent

When you voluntarily enroll in a 3QInnova messaging program, we may collect and use your mobile number, messages, and consent records to provide that program. Visiting this page or giving us your number for another purpose does not by itself enroll you in recurring messages.

Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Consent to a 3QInnova program does not authorize another business to send you messages.

Providers that transmit messages or support our communications systems may process the information necessary to perform those services on our behalf. This does not permit them to use your consent for their own marketing.

Reply STOP to the program's sending number to opt out, or HELP for assistance. You may also contact will@3qinnova.com. A final message may confirm an opt-out. Some consent or suppression records may be retained to prevent unwanted messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See our Messaging Terms & Conditions for program details.

5. Service providers and other disclosures

We may use hosting, email, cloud, and communications providers to operate our website and services. Information shared with a provider should be limited to what is needed for that service and handled under the applicable provider arrangements.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal request, or where reasonably necessary to address fraud, protect rights, or maintain service security. We may also share information at your direction. These circumstances do not grant permission to share mobile consent for third-party marketing.

6. Website hosting, cookies, and analytics

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information under its own privacy practices. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information.

This draft page does not add an analytics service, advertising tracker, sign-up form, or cookie-based consent mechanism. If 3QInnova introduces tools that collect additional information, we will update the relevant disclosures and choices as appropriate. Your browser may provide controls for cookies and other local storage used by websites you visit.

7. Retention and security

We intend to keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, an active business relationship, support, consent management, or applicable obligations. The appropriate period may differ by record type.

We intend to use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards suited to the information and service. No storage system or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Ordinary SMS is not a secure channel for confidential information.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to provide optional information, stop participating in a messaging program, or ask us to review information you previously shared. Contact will@3qinnova.com to request access, correction, or deletion, or to ask a question about our use of information.

We may need to verify a request before responding. We will consider requests under applicable requirements and any legitimate retention needs. An opt-out or deletion request does not necessarily require deletion of the minimal record needed to honor your communication preferences.

9. Children and external services

The consulting website and business messaging programs described here are intended for business contacts and authorized demonstration participants, not children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided information through these channels so we can review and address it.

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy policies govern information you provide directly to them. Separate 3QInnova products may have their own audience and privacy disclosures.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this page as our services and information-handling practices change. The update date appears at the top. Where applicable requirements call for additional notice or consent, we will provide that notice or request that consent.
