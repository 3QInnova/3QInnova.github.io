Privacy Policy

SMS/MMS terms · Website terms · Consulting terms · Privacy policy

On this page

Overview and scope 3QInnova LLC provides technology consulting and engineering services. This policy explains how we handle personal information associated with our website, business inquiries, and customer service communications, including SMS and MMS where offered. It does not replace confidentiality, security, or data-processing terms in a client agreement. A separate product or service privacy notice, where provided, describes that product or service.

1. SMS/MMS privacy and consent We use your mobile number and messaging preferences to provide customer service updates and support messages you request or agree to receive. Mobile information, SMS/MMS opt-in information, and consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Necessary messaging providers and carriers may process this information to deliver messages, maintain consent and opt-out preferences, and operate the messaging service.

Providing a phone number alone is not consent to receive automated text messages. The website’s SMS/MMS checkbox is optional and is not preselected. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help when the messaging service is available, or contact will@3qinnova.com. We may retain the minimum information necessary to honor an opt-out and document your communication preferences. See https://www.3qinnova.com/SMS_MMS_Terms/ for messaging terms.

2. Information you provide We limit the business contact information we retain to essential details supplied directly by you or by a company representative communicating with us: your name, company, email address, telephone number, and communication preferences, as applicable. We also retain necessary correspondence, such as an inquiry, support request, or related business communication. We do not purchase contact lists or obtain personal profiles from data brokers.

Please provide only information needed for your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through the general contact form, email, or text message.

3. How we use information We use essential contact details and necessary correspondence to respond to inquiries, provide requested support, discuss or administer a business relationship, and communicate about agreed services. Where messaging is offered, we use relevant preferences and consent records to manage those communications. We may also use the minimum necessary records to protect our systems, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use contact information for unrelated advertising profiles or sell access to our contacts.

4. No sale of personal information; limited disclosure 3QInnova does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not share personal information for third parties’ own marketing, targeted advertising, or promotional purposes.

Limited information may be processed by providers needed to operate website hosting, email, and SMS/MMS delivery. That processing supports the service you use; it is not a sale of your contact information. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. This policy does not authorize unrelated reuse of your information by another company.

5. Website hosting and the contact form Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. GitHub’s own privacy statement describes its processing: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

The current contact form prepares an email in your email application using the information and consent choices you enter. You must send that email to complete the request. The form does not itself submit information to a 3QInnova contact database, automatically enroll you in messaging, or create a server-side consent record. Once you send the email, it becomes correspondence handled through email providers. The prepared email includes the contact details, optional message, consent choices, and related form details.

The current website does not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting, email, messaging providers, carriers, and linked websites may process technical information under their own applicable notices. This policy does not promise that those providers collect no technical data.

6. Retention We retain essential contact details and necessary correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, support request, business relationship, or applicable legal obligation. We seek to avoid retaining unnecessary personal information and delete information that is no longer needed, subject to legitimate legal, dispute-resolution, and recordkeeping requirements. Minimal consent or suppression records may be retained to document messaging choices and prevent further messages after an opt-out. Provider backups and technical logs may follow the provider’s applicable retention practices.

7. Your choices and privacy requests You may contact will@3qinnova.com to ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or change communication preferences. We will review the request and respond as required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity and may retain limited information when necessary to comply with law, preserve relevant business records, or honor an opt-out. You do not need to opt in to SMS/MMS to contact us by email.

8. Security We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information we handle. No website, email system, text messaging service, or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Please use an agreed secure channel if an engagement requires confidential or sensitive information.

9. Changes and contact We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The published version should show its effective date. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to 3QInnova LLC at will@3qinnova.com.
