CritterRX support

Email will@3qinnova.com. Include your iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, Apple codes, sign-in tokens, private invitation links or full care records.

Signing in

Use Continue with Apple with the same Apple Account you used before. CritterRX does not offer password sign-in. If you cannot access your Apple Account, use Apple’s account-recovery service; support cannot bypass Apple authentication.

Joining an animal’s care

Install CritterRX and return to the owner’s invitation page. Open the invitation, sign in with your own Apple Account, and explicitly accept. A link expires after 24 hours and can be used by one caregiver. Ask the owner for a new link if it expired or was cancelled.

Missing or stale reminders

Open Settings → Reminders and review your animal’s reminder choices. Allow the relevant iPhone permissions. Wake-up alarms are optional and have limited advance coverage; open the app before coverage ends. Ordinary notifications follow Focus and Silent Mode settings.

An offline, force-quit or low-power phone may retain an old alarm after another caregiver records care. Reconnect and check the shared journal and the other caregiver before giving another dose. Stopping or snoozing an alarm does not record an administration.

Unconfirmed updates

An action saved on your phone is not a shared confirmation. Keep the app and its data until the action syncs or is reviewed. Reconnect and open Unconfirmed updates. Do not create a replacement report merely because an upload is pending.

Report abusive content or a caregiver

Email support with the caregiver name and the content that needs review, including only the necessary details. Owners can remove access in People. You can also block an account in the app.

Account deletion

In CritterRX, open Settings → Profile & account → Delete account. Review who will receive each shared animal. You can choose an already linked caregiver or review the randomly selected caregiver. An animal with no other linked caregiver and its private care history are permanently deleted with the account. Shared historical reports retained for other caregivers use a neutral deleted-caregiver attribution. Confirm only after reviewing the full list.
