CritterRX privacy policy

Updated November 15, 2026.

This policy covers the forthcoming public release. During pre-release testing, contact will@3qinnova.com for privacy or deletion requests while release configuration is completed.

3QInnova provides CritterRX, a shared animal-medication journal. Contact will@3qinnova.com about privacy or support.

Information used to provide care

We process your Apple sign-in identifier and the name and email Apple makes available, your chosen caregiver profile and optional photo, animal details and photos, medication instructions and schedules, dose reports and corrections, sharing and ownership relationships, and notification preferences. Apple may provide a private relay email. We do not receive your Apple password.

Account and care records are stored using Supabase. Access is limited to the relevant animal’s owner and currently authorized caregivers. Sharing a bearer invitation privately is your responsibility; the label on a link does not restrict who can redeem it. Support may access information necessary to resolve an issue or protect the service; do not include unnecessary care information in support messages.

Device data and notifications

Installation identifiers and Apple push/Live Activity tokens support synchronization and alerts. Device verification uses Apple App Attest when available. iPhone caches and drafts allow offline access; queued reports remain associated with the account that created them. Apple controls notification and background execution. Revoking online access cannot erase a copy previously downloaded to an offline device immediately.

Advertising

CritterRX uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent system. Ad requests are configured as nonpersonalized and publisher first-party IDs are disabled. Animal names, medication details, notes, caregiver identities and care history are not supplied for ad targeting. Google may process device/network information, advertising identifiers where permitted, and ad interaction/diagnostic information under its own practices. Review or change available choices in Profile → Ads & privacy.

Google advertising privacy information. Nonpersonalized ads do not mean no device information is processed. We do not sell your care records to advertisers.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Care records remain while needed to provide the shared journal. Owners control animal access; removing a caregiver preserves the factual shared audit history. Expired invitations, device metadata and notification diagnostics may be retained for security, retries and operational troubleshooting.

You can initiate account deletion in Profile → Delete account. Before confirmation, the app shows ownership transfers and any animals that will be permanently deleted. You can select a linked caregiver for each shared animal; if you do not select one, a random eligible linked caregiver is shown for review. Animals without another linked caregiver and their private care history are permanently deleted. Your profile, photos, sign-in access and device registrations are removed. Shared historical reports needed by remaining caregivers retain a neutral deleted-caregiver attribution. Operational backups may retain deleted information until they are replaced; a restore must reapply completed deletions.

Permissions and your choices

Photo access is optional. Notifications, wake-up alarms and countdowns are optional and can be changed in the app and iPhone Settings. Caregiver access is per animal. Public-release CritterRX does not scan discharge papers or run an AI prescription interpreter. Do not use the journal to infer prescriptions or catch-up dosing.

Security and updates

We use authenticated, animal-scoped server access and encrypted network transport. No system can guarantee uninterrupted or error-free delivery. We may update this policy as the product changes and display the revised date. Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion or concerns about information you have shared.
